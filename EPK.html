bio

Like Beef is a Berlin-based noise-rock trio making gritty tunes to dance to. Inspired by the angst of La Dispute, the apathy of Sonic Youth and the atmospheric textures of Blonde Redhead, the band holds a sardonic lens to urban life and transforms everyday moments into dramatic narratives. Following the release of their debut EP late last year, Like Beef are excited to announce the release of their 2nd EP ‘FRAUD’, this November 27th with release party at Supamolly, Berlin.

The band have played beloved local venues including Hinterraum, OGH and Schokoladen, and at the Vierdaagse Festival Nijmegen 2025.
